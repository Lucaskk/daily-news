## 矛盾與不確定性

- **Qusra / West Bank:** Residents, Israeli military, settler groups and rights organizations may present different accounts of who remained, whether residents were forcibly displaced and how much the military intervened.
- **Ukraine / Salavat:** Damage, downtime and repair estimates require confirmation from satellite imagery, refinery throughput and Russian company disclosures.
- **Yemen:** Houthi claims against Jazan and Saudi / U.S. responses need verification; humanitarian figures can change as aid access shifts.
- **Dominican Republic / Cuba:** Dominican authorities and Cuba differ on whether nine or ten officials were expelled; motive was not publicly explained by Dominican authorities.
- **Ryanair / NTSB:** Bird remains and prior inspections are facts in the preliminary record, but NTSB has not assigned final cause or responsibility.
- **Tariff transshipment:** The US$19B-US$26B range is a White House estimate and may be disputed by affected countries, importers or independent trade economists.
- **AI model benchmarks:** Google, xAI and OpenAI claims are provider-published; cross-model ranking should wait for independent, task-specific evaluation.
- **Flock Safety guardrails:** Flock says the controls strengthen accountability; ACLU and other critics argue structural surveillance and police-control problems remain.

## 矛盾與不確定性

- Congo Ebola numbers are still moving; WHO / AP figures are treated as current as of AP publication, not final epidemiological totals。
- Ukraine Novorossiysk damage reports combine Ukrainian claims, Russian air-defense claims and port / grain terminal reports; satellite or maritime data should be used for follow-up。
- Gaza strike identity and civilian impact are based on Israel military claim in AP reporting; Hamas / Gaza health authorities may provide conflicting accounts later。
- Pentagon Yemen civilian-casualty report is official U.S. review; local and human-rights group counts may differ by methodology。
- Panama canal $4m queue-jump figure is a reported market transaction; verify with canal authority or shipping auction data if using for quantitative analysis。
- Apple publisher-payment talks are reported by WSJ and summarized by Reuters / TradingView; Apple has not officially announced agreements。
- Google, GitHub, LangChain and OpenAI date-level posts are normalized to the window start in displays and should not be cited as exact second-level publication times。
